## What happens next
**H2:** What happens next

1. You instruct us by the form or by email.
2. We confirm availability and next steps the same business day.
3. The first attempt is typically within 24 hours of receiving instructions.
4. We record each attempt. If the person avoids service, we adjust the approach and keep you informed.
5. You receive proof of service that records when, where and how service was completed.
