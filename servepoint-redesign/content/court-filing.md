# Copy: Court filing

## Intro
**H1:** Court filing and registry services in Vancouver, BC

**Lead:** ServePoint files and issues documents at BC Supreme Court and Provincial Court registries, searches court files, retrieves copies and returns stamped documents digitally. Law firms inside and outside BC instruct us directly.

**Actions:** Request service · Call 778-238-2797

**H2:** Filing and issuing

- Filing pleadings, applications and affidavits
- Issuing Notices of Civil Claim and petitions
- Filing at BC Supreme Court and Provincial Court registries
- Registry attendance
- Digital return of filed documents, or by mail

**H2:** How to instruct

1. Send the documents, the court service you need, the deadline and your instructions, by the request form or by email.

3. We return filed documents digitally or by mail.
