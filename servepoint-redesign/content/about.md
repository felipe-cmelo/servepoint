## How a file is handled
**H2:** How a file is handled

Four commitments, each with a short heading. These are the four values on the current page (Responsiveness, Communication, Documentation, Confidentiality), written as plain statements.

- **Requests are reviewed promptly.** We confirm your deadline before we accept a file.
- **One contact knows your file.** You have a direct contact from instruction through the final affidavit of service.
- **Every attempt is recorded.** Date, time, location and the details needed for clear, accurate proof.
- **Handled with discretion.** Documents, parties and file information are kept confidential throughout.

## What we do
**H2:** What we do and what we do not do

We serve documents, file and retrieve court documents, and run BC corporate searches. We do not give legal advice. Your firm decides the method of service.
