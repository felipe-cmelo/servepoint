## Intro
**H1:** Pricing for process serving, court filing and corporate search

**Lead:** Each service starts from $168. Pricing is confirmed before service.

## The table
**Caption:** Starting prices and what is included

| Service | Starts at | Included |
|---|---|---|
| Process serving | $168 | Up to 3 service attempts, service updates, and a sworn affidavit of service on completion. If service fails after the included attempts, an affidavit of attempted service |
| Court filing and registry attendance | $168 | Filing or issuing of court documents, registry attendance, and return of filed documents by digital copy or mail |
| Corporate search | $168 | Current and historical company information, registered office and director details, and digital delivery of results |

Coverage line: Process serving covers Vancouver and the Lower Mainland. Court filing covers BC Supreme Court and Provincial Court registries. Corporate searches cover BC companies.
