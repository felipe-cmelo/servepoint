## Intro
**H1:** BC corporate search for law firms

**Lead:** ServePoint runs BC Registry searches to confirm a company's legal entity, registered office, directors and status before you file or serve. Send a company name, incorporation number or trade name. Results arrive by email, typically within 2 business days.

**Actions:** Request service · Call 778-238-2797

## Choose the search
**H2:** Choose the search

Table: Search, When you need it, What you receive.
| Search | When you need it | What you receive |
|---|---|---|
| Company summary | You need the overall picture of a company | Current registered office, directors, incorporation details and status |
| Director and officer search | You need to know who runs the company | Current directors and officers, with available address details |
| Registered office confirmation | You are about to serve a company | The company's current registered office address |
| Historical search | You need to see what changed over time | Historical corporate information and changes recorded over time |
| Certificate of status | You need confirmation of current corporate status | A certificate of the current status |
| Name and trade name search | You know an operating name but not the legal entity | The legal entity behind the business or operating name |
