## How an attempt is handled
**H2:** How an attempt is handled

1. We receive your documents and instructions and confirm availability the same business day.
2. The first attempt is typically within 24 hours. We plan attempts around the address and the circumstances of the file, and where appropriate we attempt at different times of day to improve the chance of success.
3. We record each attempt with date, time, location and relevant details.
4. We update you throughout the file.
5. When service is complete, we prepare a sworn affidavit of service.

Up to 3 attempts are included.

## When service is difficult
**H2:** When the person is hard to reach

Some addresses are secured. Some people avoid service. Some have moved, or the address on file is out of date. We record every attempt with date, time and location, and we adjust the approach and keep you informed so that your firm can consider next steps promptly.

When the defendant is a company, a BC corporate search can confirm the current registered office before further attempts. See corporate search.

If service cannot be completed after the included attempts, you receive an affidavit of attempted service and an update on next steps. The record supports whatever your firm decides to do next, such as an application for a different method of service. (The current page says the same: a record of prior attempts may help decide next steps, such as an application for substituted service. Keep the wording as a statement about the record, never as advice.)

What helps us: a physical description, working hours, vehicle information, previous attempts and building access details. Put them in the request form.
