## Specimen (sample affidavit)
Band at the top: Sample. Fictional parties, address and file number.

Heading block:
- In the Supreme Court of British Columbia
- Registry: Vancouver
- File no. S-000000 (set in the record face)
- Example Holdings Ltd., plaintiff, and Dana Whitfield, defendant
- Title: AFFIDAVIT OF PERSONAL SERVICE

Body:
I, Alex Morgan, of Vancouver, British Columbia, process server, swear that:

1. On <mark>March 4, 2026 at 9:42 a.m.</mark>, at <mark>1234 Example Street, Vancouver</mark>, I served Dana Whitfield with the Notice of Civil Claim.
2. The person confirmed their name when I asked.
3. I <mark>handed the document to the person served</mark> and left a true copy with them.
4. A copy of the document I served is attached as Exhibit A.
5. This was attempt 1 of 3. No earlier attempts were made.

Sworn before me at Vancouver, British Columbia, on March 4, 2026.

Annotations (open on hover, focus or tap; also listed under the sheet):
- When and where: the date, time and address of every attempt are recorded.
- Who: the person served is named, and how they were identified is noted.
- How: how the document was handed over is stated.
- Sworn: the affidavit is sworn before a commissioner.

Note: the annotations describe what ServePoint records. They do not state what a court requires. Do not edit them to say so.

## Step 1: Instruct
**H2:** Send the documents, the address and the deadline

Send these with your request:
- The documents to be served
- The service address, with unit or suite number, and a second address if you have one
- The deadline
- The court file number, if there is one

Use the request form, then email the documents to serve@servepointcanada.com. We confirm availability and next steps the same business day.

**Deadline this week?** Choose Rush within 48 hours or Rush same day on the form. Urgent requests are prioritized, and availability is confirmed the same business day. ## Step 4: Prove
**H2:** You receive a sworn affidavit of service

When service is complete you receive a sworn Affidavit of Service. It records when, where and how service was made.

If service cannot be completed after the included attempts, you receive an affidavit of attempted service and an update on next steps. Your firm decides what comes next. The record of each attempt supports it.

## Services
**H2:** What we do

- **Process serving.** Personal service of civil, family, commercial, witness, residential tenancy and corporate documents.
- **Court filing and registry attendance.** Filing and issuing at BC Supreme Court and Provincial Court registries, with stamped copies returned digitally.
- **BC corporate search.** Company summary, directors, registered office and status from the BC Registry. Search first, serve second.
