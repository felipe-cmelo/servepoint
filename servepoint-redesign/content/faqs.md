# FAQ bank

Source for `src/data/faqs.ts`. Each entry has an `id` and the pages that use it. Answers are 30 to 80 words.

## speed
**Q:** How quickly can documents be served?
**A:** The first attempt is typically within 24 hours of receiving your documents and instructions. Rush options are available: within 48 hours or the same day. Pricing for rush service is confirmed before service begins.
**Pages:** home, process-serving, out-of-province

## proof
**Q:** What proof of service do I receive?
**A:** A sworn Affidavit of Service when service is complete. It records when, where and how service was made. If service cannot be completed after the included attempts, you receive an affidavit of attempted service.
**Pages:** home, process-serving, out-of-province

## evasion
**Q:** What happens if the person avoids service?
**A:** Each attempt is recorded with date, time and location, and we adjust the approach and keep you informed. If service cannot be completed after the included attempts, you receive an affidavit of attempted service and an update on next steps.
**Pages:** home, process-serving, out-of-province

## out-of-province
**Q:** Do you accept out-of-province and US court documents?
**A:** Yes. We accept documents issued in other Canadian provinces and in the US, for service in Vancouver and the Lower Mainland. Your firm chooses the method of service and any court requirements. We carry out the instruction.
**Pages:** home, process-serving, out-of-province

## registered-office
**Q:** Can you confirm a company's registered office before service?
**A:** Yes. We run a BC corporate search to confirm the current registered office, then attempt service at that address. You can order the search and the service together on one instruction.
**Pages:** home, process-serving, corporate-search, out-of-province

## pricing
**Q:** How is pricing structured?
**A:** Each service starts from $168. Process serving includes up to 3 attempts and a sworn affidavit. Rush service, travel and special requests may add to the price. Pricing is confirmed before service.
**Pages:** home, process-serving, pricing

## get-started
**Q:** What do you need to get started?
**A:** The documents, the service address with unit or suite number, the deadline, and your instructions. Send them through the request form and email the documents to serve@servepointcanada.com.
**Pages:** process-serving, pricing, out-of-province

## fails
**Q:** What if service is not completed after 3 attempts?
**A:** You receive an affidavit of attempted service and an update on next steps. Your firm decides what to do next. The record of each attempt supports that decision.
**Pages:** process-serving, pricing

## filing-scope
**Q:** What can you file or issue at the registry?
**A:** Pleadings, applications and affidavits, and the issuing of Notices of Civil Claim and petitions, at BC Supreme Court and Provincial Court registries.
**Pages:** court-filing

## filing-return
**Q:** How do I receive filed documents?
**A:** Filed documents are returned digitally, or by mail if you prefer.
**Pages:** court-filing

## filing-with-service
**Q:** Can filing and service be done in one instruction?
**A:** Yes. Filing or issuing can be coordinated with service under one instruction, so filed documents move straight to service.
**Pages:** court-filing

## filing-outside-bc
**Q:** Can a firm outside BC instruct you for court work?
**A:** Yes. Firms across Canada can instruct ServePoint directly for work in Vancouver and the Lower Mainland.
**Pages:** court-filing, out-of-province

## search-types
**Q:** Which searches can you run?
**A:** Company summary, director and officer search, registered office confirmation, historical search, certificate of status, and name and trade name search.
**Pages:** corporate-search

## search-inputs
**Q:** What do you need to run a search?
**A:** A company name, an incorporation number or a trade name.
**Pages:** corporate-search

## search-turnaround
**Q:** How long does a search take?

## search-with-service
**Q:** Can I order a search and service together?
**A:** Yes. We confirm the registered office first, then attempt service. This is what we mean by search first, serve second.
**Pages:** corporate-search
