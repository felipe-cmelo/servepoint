In Vancouver, service is mostly a question of getting to the right door. Downtown, the West End, Yaletown and Gastown are dense towers. From Kitsilano to South Vancouver it is houses and suites. Each needs different information to reach the person.

We record every attempt with date, time and location, so the affidavit shows what was done at each address. Send the unit number, the deadline and when the person is reachable.
