    Common questions

    How quickly can documents be served?

    The first attempt is typically within 24 hours of receiving your documents and instructions. Rush options are available: within 48 hours or the same day. Pricing for rush service is confirmed before service begins.

    What proof of service do I receive?

    A sworn Affidavit of Service when service is complete. It records when, where and how service was made. If service cannot be completed after the included attempts, you receive an affidavit of attempted service.

    What happens if the person avoids service?

    Each attempt is recorded with date, time and location, and we adjust the approach and keep you informed. If service cannot be completed after the included attempts, you receive an affidavit of attempted service and an update on next steps.

    Do you accept out-of-province and US court documents?

    Yes. We accept documents issued in other Canadian provinces and in the US, for service in Vancouver and the Lower Mainland. Your firm chooses the method of service and any court requirements. We carry out the instruction.

    Can you confirm a company's registered office before service?

    Yes. We run a BC corporate search to confirm the current registered office, then attempt service at that address. You can order the search and the service together on one instruction.

    How is pricing structured?

    Each service starts from $168. Process serving includes up to 3 attempts and a sworn affidavit. Rush service, travel and special requests may add to the price. Pricing is confirmed before service.
