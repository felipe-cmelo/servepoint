The design direction is `docs/15-design-direction-v4.md`. `docs/06` is superseded and kept for history. Facts, URLs, SEO and data are unchanged. The copy was shortened (see below).

## What changed in v4.1 (owner feedback, 2026-11-20)
1. **Hero less techy, more law firm.** Flat navy, serif headings (Source Serif 4), no grid lines, glows, network art, glass panel or mono type, no keyboard chip in the header. The picture is a stack of paper: a fictional affidavit with a record of service and highlighted phrases that open a short note (`src/components/AffidavitSheet.astro`). "Founded by Jasmine Deo, LLB, a legal professional" sits under the actions. Inner page heroes are flat navy with a quiet "At a glance" card.
2. **Natural scrolling.** The scrubbed fades, wipes and parallax are gone. A block below the fold rises 20 px and fades in once when it comes into view, staggered when several arrive together (`IntersectionObserver`, no scroll listener, `src/scripts/scroll-engine.ts`). The header turns solid after the first pixels. The network map draws once. Page changes are a short cross fade.
3. **Phones.** Home hero with the sample sheet as a preview (Read the sample). Page leads clamp to three lines (Read more). "How a file progresses" is **pinned**: the frame sticks under the header while the reader scrolls (a scroll track of about 2.1 screens), the step bar at its top is a carousel of pills with the stage in focus always centred, each stage is a card that fills the frame (rows, a vertical timeline, tiles and outcome cards share the height), and each stage fades in over the one before, smoothly in both directions; tapping a step scrolls to it (`src/scripts/scene.ts`, `.scene-pin` and `.scene-pinned` in `src/styles/hero.css`). Tablets, landscape phones and reduced motion keep the stages in a column under a sticky step bar. Service tabs swipe. The area check is a typeahead plus one swipe row of communities and eight neighbourhood pills with Show all (the schematic map is hidden below 640 px). Card groups are swipe rows with dots. The request form folds its optional fields. The bottom bar steps aside while the hero actions or the closing band are on screen, on the request page and in short landscape windows. The two hero buttons stack, centred, 16 rem wide with a 14 px gap. The footer links are accordions. The guide contents list is a disclosure. Tap targets are at least 44 px. Patterns are in `docs/15` section 9.
4. **Buttons.** Restored to the v4 buttons at the owner's request (gradient, sheen sweep, glow, lift, teal on navy). Do not restyle them.
5. **Shorter copy.** Every page lead, section lead, card, FAQ answer, city page note, form hint and explorer line was tightened. The page and data files now hold the final wording (`src/pages`, `src/data/faqs.ts`, `src/data/pricing.ts`, `src/content/coverage`). `content/*.md` keeps the earlier drafts and is no longer the wording source. Facts, qualifiers ("typically"), the "your firm chooses the method of service" statement, "pricing is confirmed before service" and the footer disclaimer are unchanged. Legal pages and the three guides (drafts) were not edited.
6. **Search field removed from the header** at the owner's request. The command palette still opens with Ctrl or Cmd K and with the slash key.
